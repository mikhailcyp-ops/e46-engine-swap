# BMW E46 318i (M43B19) to M54B30 from an X5

Two documents. One describes the car. The other is what actually worked.

## What is on the car

[Что стоит на кузове E46 и на косах](docs/photos_ebox_wiring_analysis.md)

Photos of the E46 E-box rack, the removed M43 harness, and the X5 M54 harness, with the connector names that match those photos. The links used while naming them are at the end of that file. It does not say how to join the wires.

## The procedure

What was done on this car and worked. An E46 318i that had an M43B19, an M54B30 with the engine harness and MS43 from an X5 E53, and the original manual gearbox kept.

The photos are inside the HTML files. Open one in a browser.

Covered in the procedure: the fuel pump not priming (relay behind the glovebox, cavity 2 white/blue to the pump, cavity 8 ignition positive on the coil), the thick red power plug left uncut, the red/white wire spliced to the 4-pin on the rack, starter control, X60004 pin 10 left in place, the electronic pedal, an immo-off flash through a sacrificed round diagnostic plug and MS4X flasher, the front driveshaft section and gearbox bracket from an E46 M54B22 or M54B25 manual, E46 M54 engine mounts, the M43 flywheel and clutch kept, the M54 oil pan and dipstick, and a different expansion tank with a hose from the left heater fitting.
