# How to swap a BMW E46 318i (M43B19) to an M54B30 from an X5

This is the procedure that already worked on the car: an E46 body that had an M43B19, and an M54B30 with its engine harness and MS43 from an X5 E53.

What sits where on the body and on the harnesses, with photos and the source list, is a separate file: [What is on the body](../photos_ebox_wiring_analysis.md). Russian: [Как свапнуть](e46-318i-m43b19-to-m54b30-x5.md).

## How this file grows

1. A new fix that worked goes in the log: date, symptom, what was joined, how it was confirmed.
2. That entry becomes the next step below. A step is not written until it has been tried on the car.
3. Photos go in [photos/](photos/) and are named in [photos/README.md](photos/README.md).

## Log of decisions that worked

### 2026-11-13 — Ignition positive for the fuel-pump relay

The starter cranks. In key position 2 the fuel pump does not run and does not build pressure. The MS43 stores code **69** (fuel pump relay).

The pump relay is behind the glovebox; the glovebox has to come off. Find it by the wire that goes to the pump: white with a blue stripe, socket cavity **2**. Do not pick it by its place in the row.

The coil was missing the positive that appears with the ignition. That is socket cavity **8** (relay terminal 86, fed from terminal 87 of the DME main relay). Cavity **6** is different: constant positive from the fuse, terminal 30, and it is live with the key off.

That coil positive is made with two joints. The body plug with the thick red wire pushes into the matching plug on the engine harness. That plug is a positive distributor: one contact on one side and two on the other share one bus, and the thick red wire is not cut. Separately, the red wire with a white stripe is cut off the white plug on the harness and spliced to the red wire with a white stripe on the 4-pin plug next to the power plug on the E-box rack.

Photos are in step 1.

### 2026-11-13 — Thick black body wire to the black wire on the harness, starter

The E46 body brings a two-wire plug: a thick black wire and a thin white-green wire. The thick black wire from that plug is joined to the black wire on the black plug of the M54 harness. After that joint the starter cranks. The thin white-green wire is not part of this step.

Photos are in step 2.

### 2026-11-13 — Do not move X60004 pin 10

Pin 10 of the body X60004 plug is the wire to the fuel-pump relay. Leave it in the cavity it already occupies. At the relay the wire is the same color as where it leaves the MS43. If the relay with that color is not found, it has not been looked for hard enough.

### 2026-11-13 — Electronic throttle pedal pinout

The table gathered before the swap matched the car. The six pedal wires, X11400 to MS43 X60004: 1→7, 2→12, 3→14, 4→8, 5→9, 6→13.

### 2026-11-13 — OBD2 taken from the round under-hood plug, immo-off firmware

To flash a file with the immobilizer off (EWS off), diagnostics were brought out through the round plug under the hood. That round plug was cut and used up. An OBD2 plug from the donor car, wires already cut, was joined to the wires of the round plug. The file was written with MS4X flasher and the community patchlist.

### 2026-11-13 — Front driveshaft section and gearbox bracket, original manual gearbox kept

If the manual gearbox from the M43B19 stays in the car, the front piece of the driveshaft, from the center support bearing to the gearbox, from that engine does not fit. The front section has to come from an E46 with a six-cylinder engine and a manual gearbox. The M54B22 and M54B25 parts fit. The M54B30 part was not checked: that gearbox is thicker.

The gearbox then sits about 2 cm further back, so the old gearbox bracket does not fit either. The bracket from an E46 M54B22 or M54B25 with a manual gearbox fits. The M54B30 bracket was not checked, for the same reason.

### 2026-11-13 — Mounts, flywheel, oil pan, dipstick, heater and expansion tank

The engine mounts have to be M54 mounts from an E46. The flywheel and the clutch stay the old M43B19 parts. The oil dipstick and the oil pan come from the M54.

The heater circuit needs an extra hose, from the left heater-core fitting to the expansion tank. The tank is a different one. It needs an extra inlet on the side that faces the engine. The tank on this 318i with the M43B19 does not have that inlet, and the car has a manual gearbox.

## Steps

### 1. Give the fuel-pump relay the positive that appears with the ignition

Before the swap the in-tank pump was switched by the body relay. The MS43 still grounds the coil through body X60004 pin 10. The separate positive on that coil used to come from the M43 body. After the X5 harness is joined, that positive is missing, so in key position 2 the pump is silent even though the starter cranks.

1. Remove the glovebox. The fuel-pump relay is behind it. Find it by the wire to the pump: **white with a blue stripe**, socket cavity **2**. Do not use its place among the neighboring relays.
2. The positive that appears when the ignition is switched on belongs on cavity **8** (relay terminal 86). On this car it was missing. Cavity **6** is the other terminal: constant positive from the fuse (terminal 30). It is live with the key off.
3. On the E-box rack, find the body plug with the thick red wire. It sits where the harness meets the engine fuses. The 4-pin and the 2-pin plugs next to it on the same rack are not this power plug.

![Plugs on the E46 E-box rack. The thick red leaves the middle one](photos/16-ebox-power-plugs.jpg)

4. Unplug that connector and plug it into the matching connector on the M54 harness. Do not cut the thick red wire. One contact on the body side and two on the harness side is normal: it is one positive bus, shared by every wire plugged into it.

### 2. Bring starter control from the body

Terminal 50 is the thin wire that operates the starter solenoid, not the thick battery cable on the starter. On the E46 body it arrives in a two-wire plug. The other wire in that plug is thin white-green, and the starter does not use it.

1. Find the body plug with two wires: thick black and thin white-green.
2. On the M54 harness, find the black plug and the black wire in it.

![Black plug of the engine harness, wire side](photos/12-black-plug-wires.jpg)

![The same plug from the side, with the stripped black wire](photos/13-black-plug-side.jpg)

3. Join the thick black wire from the body to the black wire of that black plug. Insulate the joint.

![Stripped thick black wire at the black plug](photos/14-starter-black-open.jpg)

![Starter black-wire joint in the E-box](photos/15-starter-black-joint.jpg)

After this, the key in the start position cranks the engine.

### 3. Do not move pin 10 of the fuel-pump relay

X60004 pin 10 is the fuel-pump relay control, the MS43 pulling the coil to ground. It is not the positive feed to the pump motor. The positive on the coil is step 1.

Leave the body contact in cavity 10. It does not get moved. The wire arrives at the fuel-pump relay in the same color it has where it leaves the ECU. Find the relay by that color, not by its place in the row. If no relay has a wire of that color, the search is not finished.

### 4. Connect the electronic accelerator pedal

The M43 pedal was a cable. On the MS43 the six wires of the electronic pedal, X11400, land on the body X60004 in the table that was written before the swap and then matched the car.

| Pedal X11400 | MS43 X60004 | Function |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 7 | Sensor 1 ground |
| 2 | 12 | Sensor 2 ground |
| 3 | 14 | Sensor 2 5 V supply |
| 4 | 8 | Sensor 1 signal |
| 5 | 9 | Sensor 1 5 V supply |
| 6 | 13 | Sensor 2 signal |

The sensor supply comes from the MS43. Do not put 12 V on the pedal signal wires or on the 5 V wires.

### 5. Bring out an OBD2 plug and flash the EWS-off file

The stock diagnostic connection was not enough to flash the MS43. The round plug under the hood was cut and its wires were used. An OBD2 plug was taken from the donor car with its wires already cut. The round-plug wires were joined to the wires of that OBD2 plug.

Through the resulting plug, a file with immo off (EWS off) was written. The program is MS4X flasher. The patch set is the community patchlist.

### 6. Replace the front driveshaft section and the gearbox bracket

This step is for the case where the manual gearbox from the M43B19 stays in the car.

The front piece of the driveshaft, from the center support bearing to the gearbox, from the M43B19 is too short and does not mate. The rear piece, from the center bearing back, is not changed in this step.

The front section has to come from an E46 with a six-cylinder engine and a manual gearbox. Parts from an M54B22 and from an M54B25 fit. Do not take an M54B30 part on faith: the B30 gearbox is thicker, and that front section was not tried against the 318i gearbox.

After the M54 is installed the gearbox sits about 2 cm further toward the rear of the car. The bracket that held the gearbox on the M43 body does not reach the new place. The bracket also comes from an E46 M54B22 or M54B25 with a manual gearbox. The M54B30 bracket was not checked, for the same reason as the driveshaft.

### 7. Mounts, flywheel, oil pan, dipstick, and the heater circuit

The engine mounts are M54 mounts from an E46 body. X5 mounts are not made for this body.

The flywheel stays the M43B19 flywheel. The clutch stays the M43B19 clutch.

The oil dipstick comes from the M54. The oil pan comes from the M54.

Add a heater hose from the left heater-core fitting to the expansion tank. Change the tank for one that has an extra inlet on the side facing the engine, for that hose. The 318i tank from the M43B19 does not have that inlet, including on a car with a manual gearbox.
