# Stock E46 engines (reference)

Factory engines you are most likely to encounter on a used E46. Power figures vary slightly by market (EU vs US) and year.

## Four-cylinder

| Code | Displacement | Typical models | Notes |
|------|----------------|----------------|--------|
| M43 | 1.9 L | Early 318i | Older; less common swap base |
| N42 / N46 | 2.0 L | 318i/320i (later) | Valvetronic; different electronics than M54 era |

## Six-cylinder (most swap-relevant)

| Code | Displacement | Typical models | Notes |
|------|----------------|----------------|--------|
| M52TU | 2.5 / 2.8 L | 323i, 328i | TU = technical update; vanos, drive-by-wire throttle on later |
| M54 | 2.2 / 2.5 / 3.0 L | 325i, 330i, Z4 overlap | Very common; good documentation; **M54B30** popular upgrade from smaller six |
| S54 | 3.2 L | M3 only | High-rev NA; expensive; OEM “max” swap for many RWD E46s |

## Diesel (where sold)

| Code | Notes |
|------|--------|
| M47 | 318d/320d — different fuel system, wiring, and weight; rarely a performance swap target |

## Transmissions & differentials (pairing matters)

- **Manual**: match clutch, flywheel, and bellhousing to engine; check slave cylinder style.
- **Auto**: torque converter and TCU/DME coordination; manual conversion is non-trivial.
- **Final drive**: ratio affects acceleration vs fuel; document tag on differential.

## Common *within-BMW* upgrade paths

1. **M54B25 → M54B30**: harness/DME/instrument coding, exhaust manifolds, possibly drive-by-wire pedal if coming from older M52.
2. **Any six → S54**: oil pan/subframe clearance, exhaust, cooling, DME/EWS, drive flange; often considered the “OEM+” ceiling on RWD E46.
3. **Supercharger/turbo on M54**: keeps BMW integration; heat management and fueling become the limit.

Document your **current** engine code (sticker on valve cover / VIN / ISTA) before choosing a path.
